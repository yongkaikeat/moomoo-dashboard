How a trade happens

    The AI's risk advice

    Each morning before the market opens, the AI research analyst reads the overnight news and the 8:30 New York time economic data. It can then recommend less risk or to avoid a fund, and it must name the news it used. It can never ask for more risk. The advice is used only if a person approves it in Slack before 9:55 New York time; a person can also choose a different cut, from 0 to 100%.
